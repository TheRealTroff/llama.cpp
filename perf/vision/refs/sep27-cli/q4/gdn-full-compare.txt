This diagram compares the internal architectures of two large language models (LLMs):

1.  **Qwen3 Next 80B-A3B** (left side, teal/blue)
2.  **Kimi Linear 48B-A3B** (right side, blue)

Both are Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) models with a similar high-level structure: token embedding → multiple transformer layers (each containing an attention mechanism and a MoE block) → final RMSNorm → linear output layer.

---

## Main Architectural Difference

The **primary architectural difference** lies in the **type of attention mechanism used within each transformer layer**, specifically how they handle long-range dependencies and computational efficiency.

### Qwen3 Next 80B-A3B:
- Uses a **hybrid attention scheme** with a **3:1 ratio** of linear attention to full attention.
- In every group of 4 layers:
  - Layers 1, 2, 3 → **Gated DeltaNet** (a form of *linear attention*)
  - Layer 4 → **Gated Attention** (standard scaled dot-product attention)
- The “Gated DeltaNet” is a novel linear attention variant that uses convolutional components and gating mechanisms (σ = Sigmoid, ψ = SiLU, φ = Softplus) to model state updates efficiently.
- This design aims for **linear complexity** in sequence length for most layers, while retaining some full attention for critical context modeling.

- Also uses a **3:1 ratio** of linear attention to full attention.
